# How to start playing PokéSurvivor

[Back to the game overview](../README.md) · [Play PokéSurvivor](https://survivorpoke.com/)

PokéSurvivor is a free browser survival game: choose a Pokémon partner, avoid enemy hordes while your active move attacks automatically, collect experience and choose rewards to strengthen your run. You can start without downloading an app or registering an account. The game interface supports English and Spanish.

## Your first run

1. Open [survivorpoke.com](https://survivorpoke.com/) and press **Press to start**. The game restores this browser's account or creates an anonymous account with a public nickname.
2. On a new save, answer the personality quiz to meet your first partner. Later, use **Collection** to select another Pokémon you own.
3. Start a solo run. Move with **WASD** or the **arrow keys**. On a phone, rotate to landscape and drag a finger on an open area of the game.
4. Keep an escape route through open ground. Attacks fire automatically; positioning determines whether you can survive and land useful hits.
5. Collect experience dropped by enemies. At each level-up, choose one of the three offered cards.
6. When finished, open the solo pause screen with **Esc/P** (or the pause icon) and choose **Quit run**. Review the result and choose **Back to menu**. Spend earned currency or choose another partner, and check that your progress has synced before leaving.

The [full controls guide](controls-and-mobile.md) explains keyboard shortcuts, the floating touch joystick and pause behavior.

## Choose the attack that actually fires

You can hold up to **four ordinary move slots**, but only the **active slot** attacks. Press **1–4**, cycle with **Q/E**, or click/tap a move icon to select it. A newly learned move is normally selected automatically, so check the highlighted icon if damage changes after a reward.

Buff moves strengthen your Pokémon but do not directly damage enemies. Switch to a damaging move when needed. A **Bond** can combine multiple moves in one active group; the moves in that group can act together.

## Pick rewards for the next problem

| Run card | Effect | Useful when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vigor | +20 maximum HP and restores the added health | You need room for mistakes |
| Sitrus Berry | Heals 45% of maximum HP immediately | You are already hurt |
| Sprint | +7% movement speed | You need space or an escape route |
| Power | +10% damage with all moves | Your current attack needs more damage |
| Reflexes | 8% shorter move cooldown | More frequent attacks help your build |

These cards last for the current run. Some also unlock [move evolutions](move-evolutions.md): for example, level-5 Ember plus the **Power card chosen in that run** makes Fire Blast available as a gold evolution reward.

## Rifts, Bonds and later encounters

Legendary rifts become eligible after **75 seconds**. Eligible checks occur every **30 seconds**, with opening chance rising from 20% after missed eligible rolls. A live ordinary boss can block an attempt, so these timings do not promise a portal at an exact second.

When a purple portal appears, enter within **45 seconds** and defeat the legendary within the **120-second** arena limit. Victory grants a Bond and encounter rewards. Use a Bond to pair compatible loose moves, then expand an unfinished group with later Bonds to a maximum of **four moves in that group**. No new rifts open from minute **28**; Arceus's Judgment begins at minute **30**.

If a rift times out or the party loses, you return without the victory reward and the main run continues. See [legendary rifts and Bonds](https://survivorpoke.com/guides/legendary-rifts/) for the complete conditions.

## Build your permanent collection

Tickets buy pulls in nine regional banners, from Kanto through Paldea. Base 5★ chance is **1.5%**; a banner's **70th pull** guarantees 5★ if no earlier 5★ reset its counter. Counters are separate for each region. A ×10 pull guarantees at least one **4★ or higher**.

Coins buy upgrades for the **selected Pokémon**, rather than the entire collection. For example, the first Vitality level costs **60 coins** and grants **+8 maximum HP**. Permanent shop upgrades do not replace the current-run cards required for move evolution. Find rates, duplicate refunds, shinies, rescue rewards and all upgrades in [collection and gacha](https://survivorpoke.com/guides/collection-and-gacha/).

## Keep progress and find your rank

Your account retains collection, currency, tickets, upgrades, achievements and completed results. **Refreshing ends the current battle**; live move levels and battlefield state are not a resumable save. Read [save and sync](save-and-sync.md) before changing browsers or devices.

Completed runs of at least **60 seconds** can enter the leaderboard. Survival time determines rank; Solo and Group boards are separate. Check the relevant period and **All-time** if you are outside a period's top 50. Finish normally and allow the record upload to complete before refreshing. [Leaderboard rules](https://survivorpoke.com/guides/leaderboard/) explain filters and missing records.
