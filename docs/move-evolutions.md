# PokéSurvivor move evolutions: all 59 recipes

[Back to the game overview](../README.md) · [Play PokéSurvivor](https://survivorpoke.com/)

To evolve a compatible move, raise it to **level 5**, take its required **run-stat card in the same battle**, then select the **gold evolution reward** at a level-up choice. These upgrades change your move, rather than evolving your Pokémon into another species.

## How to evolve Ember into Fire Blast

1. Learn Ember with a Pokémon that can receive it.
2. Select Ember upgrade cards until Ember reaches **level 5**.
3. Select at least one **Power** card during that run. You can take Power before or after reaching move level 5.
4. At a level-up choice, select the gold **Fire Blast** evolution card when offered.
5. Check the active slot so the evolved attack is the one you are using.

Meeting the requirements makes the reward eligible; it does not silently evolve the move. A permanent damage upgrade or held-item bonus is not the Power card required by this recipe. The same distinction applies to every card below.

## The cards that count

| Run card | Effect in the current run |
| --- | --- |
| Vigor | +20 maximum HP, with the added health restored |
| Power | +10% damage with all moves |
| Sprint | +7% movement speed |
| Reflexes | 8% shorter move cooldown |
| Armor | +6 percentage points of damage reduction, subject to the run cap |
| Synthesis | +0.9 HP regenerated per second |
| Magnet | +30% experience pickup radius |
| Learning | +12% experience gained |

These effects describe reward cards selected during a battle. A shop upgrade with a similar name, a high natural stat or an equipped item does not substitute for that card. Learning is included for card recognition; no recipe in the current 59-row table requires it.

## Why a move has not evolved

- **The move is missing from the table:** it has no recipe in this list. Buff moves cannot be forced into an evolution.
- **The move has not reached level 5:** take its own upgrade cards rather than unrelated rewards.
- **The exact card is missing:** select the named current-run card. Relevant prerequisite cards are marked when a maximum-level move is waiting.
- **Both conditions are met:** you still have to select the gold evolution reward when offered.
- **You bought a shop upgrade:** that improves permanent stats but does not satisfy the run-card requirement.
- **The move is already evolved:** the evolved form does not receive a second evolution through this system.

For a second example, level-5 **Bubble + Sprint** unlocks Surf; level-5 **Confusion + Reflexes** unlocks Psyshock. Evolving a move does not automatically add a new species to your collection.

Read the [on-site evolution guide](https://survivorpoke.com/guides/move-evolutions/) beside your run. The separate Wiki's [evolution checker](https://poke-survivor.org/tools/evolution-checker/) lets you enter a move, level and cards to find a missing requirement; it does not read or modify your live battle.
