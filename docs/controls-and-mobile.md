# PokéSurvivor controls and mobile play

[Back to the game overview](../README.md) · [Play PokéSurvivor](https://survivorpoke.com/)

Move to stay out of the horde, then select the active move that suits the enemies around you. Attacks fire automatically from that active slot. Keyboard, mouse and touchscreen controls are available; the mobile game is designed for landscape play.

## Keyboard and mouse

| Input | Action | Context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WASD or arrow keys | Move your Pokémon | During a battle |
| 1–4 | Select an available active move slot | During a battle |
| Q / E | Previous / next move slot | During a battle |
| Click a move icon | Select that slot | On the battle HUD |
| Esc / P | Open or close pause | Pauses solo play |
| 1–3 | Select a displayed reward card | During a level-up choice |

The number keys change meaning when a reward choice is open. Finish choosing the card, then check the highlighted move icon. Owning four ordinary moves does not make all four attack together. A Bonded group can contain several moves acting within the same active slot.

## Start a mobile run

1. Open [the game](https://survivorpoke.com/) in your mobile browser and press to start.
2. Turn your phone or tablet to **landscape**. Disable device rotation lock if the portrait prompt remains.
3. Select your partner and begin. Touch an open area of the battlefield and drag to move.
4. Release your finger to stop. Start the next drag wherever feels comfortable.
5. Tap an attack icon at the bottom to switch moves. Tap a reward card when the level-up choice appears.
6. Tap the pause icon at the upper right to open the pause screen.

The joystick appears where your drag begins, so you do not need to find a fixed joystick button. **Settings → Controls → Joystick size** offers Small, Normal and Large. This changes the drag distance used by the control, rather than your Pokémon's movement-speed stat.

## Fullscreen and a home-screen shortcut

Use **Settings → Graphics → Fullscreen** when your browser offers it. Browser and device support determine whether fullscreen and automatic landscape locking are available.

On an iPhone or iPad where web fullscreen is unavailable, open the game in Safari and use **Share → Add to Home Screen**. On Android, use the browser's Install or Add to Home Screen option when offered. You can also open [the dedicated game address](https://survivorpoke.com/play/).

A shortcut opens the game; it is not an account backup. Regular tabs, shortcuts and private windows can have separate login storage. Keep the account you already use and review [save protection](save-and-sync.md) before moving to another context. Cloud sync, rankings and co-op still need an internet connection.

## Settings for comfort and performance

| Setting | Where to find it | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Auto / High / Medium / Low graphics | Settings → Graphics | Adjust effects and particles |
| Less shakes and flashes | Settings → Graphics | Reduce camera shake and flash intensity |
| Show FPS | Settings → Graphics | Display an on-screen performance readout |
| English / Español | Settings → Graphics | Change the in-game interface language |
| Music and sound | Settings → Sound | Adjust or mute audio |

If a crowded scene feels slow, start with **Auto** or **Low** and reduce shakes and flashes. These settings do not remove permanent upgrades or change move-evolution requirements. Keep the game tab visible during an active battle.

## Fix unresponsive movement or attack switching

1. Finish any open reward, held-item or Bond choice. Some decision screens intentionally hold movement.
2. Click or tap the game after reading another tab or guide to return focus.
3. Release and press the movement key again. Held keys are cleared when the game loses focus.
4. On touch, lift your finger and begin a fresh drag outside HUD buttons.
5. If **1–4**, **Q/E** or attack-icon selection appears blocked, check your held item. **Choice Band** prevents ordinary move switching.
6. If input is delayed along with heavy effects, lower graphics quality.

In co-op, your ordinary pause screen does **not** freeze the whole shared battle. Shared card, item or Bond decisions can hold the group while choices are resolved. Stay aware of enemies when opening your menu.

Before reloading, check the save status: a refresh ends the live run and cannot restore its battlefield. Clearing browser data is especially risky for an unbound anonymous account.
