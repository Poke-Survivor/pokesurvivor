## Artwork visible in screenshots

The Pokémon sprites visible in the game come from [SpriteCollab](https://github.com/PMDCollab/SpriteCollab), with individual artists identified in the complete game credits. The upstream project identifies this artwork as **Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial 4.0**. View the [CC BY-NC 4.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).

The PNGs in `media/` are captures of the hosted game interface and gameplay. They are illustrative screenshots, not a sprite pack, and they do not grant a new or broader license to the underlying artwork. Game lettering uses **Pixelify Sans**, covered by the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://survivorpoke.com/assets/fonts/OFL.txt). No font files are included here.
